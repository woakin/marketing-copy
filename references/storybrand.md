# StoryBrand (SB7) for Websites
**Core Concept:** On a high-converting website, the visitor is the Hero; your product or company is the Guide. If you position yourself as the hero, visitors leave.

---

### The 7 Website StoryBrand Elements

1. **The Hero (The Visitor):**
   - The headline and above-the-fold content must immediately reflect what the visitor wants to achieve.
   - Speak directly to their aspirations and desired transformation.

2. **The Problem (External, Internal & Philosophical):**
   - **External:** The tangible friction (e.g., *"Reports take 15 hours every week"*).
   - **Internal:** How that friction makes them feel (e.g., *"Exhausted, second-guessing every Monday presentation"*).
   - **Philosophical:** Why this is fundamentally unfair (e.g., *"Talented operators shouldn't be trapped copying and pasting spreadsheet rows"*).
   - **The Villain:** The root antagonist (e.g., *"Fragile legacy tools and broken data silos"*).

3. **The Guide (Your Brand):**
   - **Empathy:** Prove you understand their day-to-day struggle (*"We've been in your shoes..."*).
   - **Authority:** Establish trust without bragging: customer logos, satisfaction ratings, data processed, or industry badges.

4. **The Plan (The 3-Step Process Section):**
   - Remove the fear of complexity. Break onboarding into 3 clear, sequential steps:
     1. *Step 1: Sign up in 60 seconds (no code required)*
     2. *Step 2: Sync your existing tools*
     3. *Step 3: Enjoy automated clarity on autopilot*

5. **Calls to Action (Primary vs. Transitional):**
   - **Primary CTA:** High-intent conversion path (*"Start Free Trial"* / *"Book Your Strategy Call"*). Placed in the sticky header, hero, and page footer.
   - **Transitional CTA:** Low-friction path for visitors who aren't ready to buy yet (*"Explore Live Demo"* / *"View Interactive Calculator"*).

6. **Avoid Failure (The Cost of Inaction):**
   - Explicitly highlight what is at stake if they keep the status quo: wasted budget, burned-out teams, falling behind competitors.

7. **Character Transformation & Success:**
   - Paint a vivid, believable picture of life with your solution: confident decisions, automated systems, reclaimed weekends.

---

### Website Verification Checklist

- [ ] Does the above-the-fold copy pass the 3-Second Test (answers what it is, who it is for, and how to get it)?
- [ ] Is the visitor positioned as the Hero and the company as the Guide?
- [ ] Are both Primary and Transitional CTAs clearly distinguished?
- [ ] Is the 3-Step Plan simple enough to grasp in 5 seconds?
- [ ] Are the stakes of inaction clearly communicated?
